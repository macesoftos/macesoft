# ZenshoTech Getting Started: Complete System Tutorial

Target length: approximately 22 minutes

## Description

Learn how to set up and use ZenshoTech, including business settings, branches, staff, clients, appointments, POS, inventory, marketing, reports, subscriptions, and mobile access.

## Recording shot list and narration

### 00:00 — Welcome to ZenshoTech

**Shot:** Clean ZenshoTech sign-in page, then a brief title card.

**Narration:**

“Welcome to ZenshoTech. In this tutorial, I’ll show you how to set up your workspace and complete the main daily workflows, from adding clients and appointments to processing sales and viewing reports. ZenshoTech is designed for aesthetic clinics and service businesses that want appointments, client records, point of sale, staff, inventory, marketing, and reporting in one secure workspace. We’ll use fictional demo information throughout this video, and no real client or payment data will be shown.”

### 00:45 — Signing in and the first-login tour

**Shot:** Enter the demo email. Pause or obscure the recording while entering the password. Open the dashboard welcome dialog, start the tour, move through several steps, demonstrate Previous, pause with Escape, resume from Getting Started, and finish the tour.

**Narration:**

“Sign in with your workspace email. Your password remains private and should never be shared or recorded. A new account receives this welcome only after the dashboard is ready. Start the quick tour to learn the controls available to your role. The highlighted area shows what each step describes, while Previous and Next let you move at your own pace. You can skip or pause at any time. Progress is saved to your account, so the tour can resume after a refresh or on another device. The tour also respects permissions: users only see guidance for modules they can actually open. You can restart it later from Getting Started, the account menu, the mobile More menu, or Help and Support.”

### 02:15 — Understanding the dashboard

**Shot:** Dashboard overview, date-range selector, KPIs, appointments, revenue, activity, inventory alerts, branch performance, Quick Actions, notifications, and collapsed/expanded Getting Started card.

**Narration:**

“The dashboard is your daily overview. Use the date selector to review recent performance. The summary cards show appointments, revenue, new clients, and open leads when those modules are included in your role. Below, you can review today’s schedule, sales trends, recent activity, stock alerts, and branch performance. Quick Actions starts common work without searching through menus. Notifications collect important updates and link back to the relevant module. New owners should begin with Getting Started. Its progress comes from real workspace records and updates automatically as setup work is completed.”

### 03:40 — Business and branch setup

**Shot:** Settings business profile, logo upload control, contact/currency fields, then Branches create/edit form with Davao Main Branch, timezone, address, contact information, operating hours, and rooms.

**Narration:**

“Start by reviewing the business settings used on receipts, client communications, and shared materials. Confirm the business name, logo, currency, tax and receipt details. Next, open Branches. We’ll configure Davao Main Branch with its address, contact details, Philippine timezone, and operating hours. Branch information controls where records belong and which locations staff can access. Add treatment rooms and other scheduling resources here so appointments can reserve the correct space. Save the branch once the details are complete.”

### 05:15 — Services, treatments, and rooms

To change it later, reopen the service and edit the details. If a service is no longer offered, deactivate it instead of deleting business history.”

### 11:30 — POS and payment workflow

**Shot:** POS with Angela selected. Add Signature Facial and Vitamin C Serum, change quantity, add a demo discount, review totals, use test mode or a non-settling demo payment, complete, open receipt, then Reports.

**Narration:**

“In POS, select Angela so the sale stays attached to her profile. Add Signature Facial, then add Vitamin C Serum from inventory. Adjust quantity if needed and apply an authorized demo discount. Review the subtotal, discount, tax, and final total before choosing a payment method. For training, use test mode or a safe non-settling method—never process a real payment in a tutorial. Complete the transaction and open the receipt. The sale now appears in client history, transaction records, dashboard revenue, and reports. Product quantities also update inventory when the configured POS rules apply.”

### 13:15 — Inventory

**Shot:** Create Vitamin C Serum with demo SKU, category, cost/price, branch, stock, reorder threshold; receive or adjust stock; show movement/history and low-stock status.

**Narration:**

“Inventory tracks retail products and consumables by branch. Add Vitamin C Serum with a demo SKU, category, unit, cost, selling price, opening stock, and reorder level. Use Receive Stock or the appropriate movement action to record adjustments with a reason. The movement history shows what changed and who recorded it. Low-stock alerts help the team reorder before an item runs out, while completed POS product sales reduce stock according to the configured workflow.”

### 16:10 — Expenses and reports

**Shot:** Add a fictional supplies expense; Reports date range and branch filters; sales, appointments, staff, inventory, marketing; CSV/print export where available.

**Narration:**

“Use Expenses to record a fictional clinic supply cost with its date, branch, category, amount, method, and approval state. ### 17:25 — Subscription and billing

**Shot:** Current Subscription and Pricing pages; monthly/annual toggle; show Starter, Growth, Unlimited limits and trial state.

**Narration:**

“The Subscription page shows your current plan, account usage, branch usage, included website pages, and trial or renewal status. Pricing can switch between monthly and annual billing, with the annual view showing the configured ten-percent saving. Starter is three thousand nine hundred pesos per month with up to ten users, one branch, and eight website pages. Growth is five thousand nine hundred pesos per month with up to fifteen users, up to three branches, and fifteen website pages. Unlimited is seven thousand nine hundred ninety-nine pesos per month with unlimited users and branches and twenty website pages. Every plan keeps the seven-day trial and no setup fee.”

**Required exclusion:** The repository currently has no Lemon Squeezy checkout or customer billing portal. Do not claim to open a portal, update a payment method, show provider billing history, cancel, or change a live subscription until that integration exists and is configured in test mode.
