# ZenshoTech clinic outreach campaign

## Recommended first-touch subject

`A simpler way to run [Clinic name]`

Preheader:

`Appointments, client records, treatments, inventory, and follow-ups in one workspace.`

## Alternative subject lines

- `Quick idea for [Clinic name]`
- `Reduce admin work at [Clinic name]`
- `[First name], is your clinic using one connected system?`

Avoid misleading reply prefixes such as `Re:` or exaggerated claims. Keep the first subject short and specific to the recipient.

## Personalization required before sending

- Replace `[First name]` with the recipient's first name.
- Replace `[Clinic name]` with the clinic or business name.
- Add ZenshoTech's complete business mailing address.
- Replace `[UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK]` with Sender's official unsubscribe merge tag.
- Confirm that replies and the CTA reach `sales@zenshotech.com`.
- Confirm the “Start Your Free Trial Today” CTA opens `https://zenshotech.com/register`.
- Confirm all five product-tour slides rotate in roughly 20 seconds total (about 4 seconds per slide).
- Upload the three exact system screenshots and the website-work collage to Sender's image library, then replace the local `assets/...` paths with Sender's hosted image URLs before sending.
- The product tour uses a CSS-only rotating slider. Email clients that remove CSS animation will show the first slide as the fallback. For universal animation, export the three system slides as an animated GIF or link to a hosted product-tour page.

## Recommended audience

Use a small, carefully researched list of decision-makers at aesthetic clinics, dermatology clinics, wellness centers, medspas, salons, and similar appointment-based businesses in the Philippines. Do not use purchased or indiscriminately scraped lists.

## Sending approach

1. Start with a small batch and review delivery, replies, bounces, and spam complaints.
2. Personalize the opening sentence for each prospect where possible.
3. Use one primary CTA: reply or request a 15-minute demonstration.
4. Send no more than two polite follow-ups when there is no response.
5. Suppress bounced, unsubscribed, or objecting contacts immediately.

## Follow-up copy

Subject: `Re: A simpler way to run [Clinic name]`

Hi [First name],

Just following up in case improving appointments, client records, treatment documentation, inventory, and follow-ups is a priority for [Clinic name].

I would be happy to give you a focused 15-minute walkthrough of ZenshoTech. If this is not relevant, just let me know and I will close the loop.

Best,  
James Pandian  
ZenshoTech  
sales@zenshotech.com

## Final quality checks

- Send a test to Gmail and Outlook before launching.
- Check the mobile preview and verify the CTA.
- Confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and custom tracking remain verified.
- Verify the unsubscribe link and mailing address appear in the received email.
- Ensure every recipient has a legitimate business relevance to the offer.
